RE4Inventory - Attache Case Grid Inventory (fan-made, non-commercial)
====================================================================

This is a non-commercial fan work (2차 창작) made as a university assignment.
It is not affiliated with or endorsed by CAPCOM. No CAPCOM game assets, logos
or data are included; all models below come from the listed licensed sources
or were made for this project.

Controls
--------
  Left mouse / Right mouse or R : pick & place / rotate
  C : cancel    X : discard held item
  Z : undo      1 : arrange (space)   2 : arrange (category)   E : expand case
  Alt+F4 : quit
